Examples:

A completed sale must reduce available inventory exactly once.

A duplicate request must not create a duplicate sale.

A serialized device cannot be sold twice.

A refund cannot exceed the refundable amount.

A customer credit balance must equal:
total credit sales - valid payments.

Stock must equal the valid movement balance.

Unauthorized users must not execute restricted operations.
