Design the complete product management interface for a professional electronics store management application.

The interface must be in French.

Products include:
- smartphones
- mobile phones
- accessories
- gadgets
- electronic equipment

The product model must support:
- product
- category
- brand
- SKU
- variant
- purchase cost
- selling price TTC
- stock
- serialized products
- IMEI 1
- IMEI 2
- serial number
- condition
- warranty
- supplier
- product status

Create the following screens:

1. Product list
2. Product detail
3. Create product
4. Edit product
5. Product variant management
6. Serialized inventory
7. IMEI detail
8. Product stock history

Product list should support:
- search
- filters
- category
- brand
- status
- stock status
- serialized/non-serialized
- sorting
- pagination

Use professional tables and clear status badges.

Create realistic examples such as:
- Apple iPhone 15
- Samsung Galaxy S25
- Xiaomi Redmi Note
- AirPods
- USB-C charger
- Power bank
- Phone case

Use FCFA prices.

Do not add an e-commerce storefront.
This is an internal business management application.
