Design the inventory management module of a professional electronics store management application.

Language: French.
Currency: FCFA.
Market: Cameroon.

The inventory system is based on stock movements.

A stock movement can be:
- purchase receipt
- sale
- customer return
- supplier return
- manual adjustment
- reservation
- release

Create:

1. Inventory dashboard
2. Inventory list
3. Stock movement history
4. Product inventory detail
5. Serialized inventory
6. IMEI history
7. Stock adjustment modal
8. Low-stock view

The product inventory detail should display:

- current stock
- available stock
- reserved stock
- product cost
- selling price
- inventory value
- movement history

For serialized products display:
- IMEI 1
- IMEI 2
- serial number
- status
- supplier
- purchase date
- sale date
- customer
- warranty

Use a professional ERP-style interface.

Prioritize traceability and clarity.

Include empty, loading, error and confirmation states.
