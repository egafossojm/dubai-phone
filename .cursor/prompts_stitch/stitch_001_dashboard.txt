Design the main dashboard of a professional French-language electronics store management application used in Cameroon.

The application manages:
- smartphones
- mobile phones
- accessories
- gadgets
- electronic equipment
- inventory
- purchases
- sales
- customers
- customer credit
- suppliers
- returns
- warranties

Currency: FCFA / XAF.

Design a desktop-first responsive dashboard for managers and administrators.

The dashboard should immediately answer:

- How much did the store sell today?
- How much did it sell this month?
- How many sales were completed?
- What is the estimated gross profit?
- What products are selling best?
- Which products have low stock?
- How much customer credit is outstanding?
- What payment methods are being used?
- Which salespeople are performing best?

Create:

1. Sidebar navigation
2. Top header
3. Store selector area
4. Global search
5. User profile menu
6. KPI cards:
   - CA aujourd'hui
   - CA du mois
   - Nombre de ventes
   - Marge estimée
   - Crédit client en cours
7. Sales trend chart
8. Top selling products
9. Low stock alerts
10. Recent sales
11. Payment method breakdown
12. Customer credit summary
13. Recent activity
14. Quick actions

Use realistic French labels and realistic FCFA amounts.

Include:
- loading state
- empty state
- error state
- responsive tablet layout

Do not invent features outside the described business requirements.
